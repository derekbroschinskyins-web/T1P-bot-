The honest version

Every recruiting page you've seen buries this part. We're putting it first, because the people it scares off were going to quit in week three anyway.

You are paid on commission. Only.

No base. No draw. No hourly. You're a 1099 independent contractor, and you get paid when business issues and places — not when you submit it. Submitted business is cool. Placed business pays.

You need a license. We don't get it for you.

Pre-licensing course, state exam, fingerprints, NPN, carrier contracting through SureLC. We walk you through every step and Brin pushes your contracting along — but you do the coursework and you sit the exam.

This is a phone job.

You start by hand dialing. Not with a power dialer, not with automation. You learn how to control a conversation, handle objections, and stay on the call. Tools help after skill. Not before.

Most people quit early.

Not because they couldn't do it. Because they stopped before the reps compounded. In our own new-agent guide it's written in plain English: most people quit before they ever get enough reps to improve.

Straight answers

The questions you were going to ask anyway

Is this an MLM?

No — and here's the honest, complete answer instead of a one-word denial.

You are never paid for recruiting somebody. You are paid commission on insurance policies that issue and place with real clients. That's the only thing that generates revenue here.

There is a build track: once you're producing at a high level, you can bring on agents and earn an override on the business they write. It's optional. Plenty of our people never touch it and just produce. There is no buy-in, no inventory, no product you purchase, and no requirement to recruit anybody, ever.

Do I need my license before I book?

No. Most people book this call unlicensed — that's the normal starting point. We'll tell you exactly what your state requires and how long it typically takes.

If you're already licensed and contracted elsewhere, say so on the call; that conversation goes differently.

What does it cost me?

We don't charge you to join, and we don't sell you a training package.

You will pay your state and your course provider for pre-licensing, the exam and fingerprinting. That's normal licensing cost for anyone entering insurance in any state, and it goes to them, not to us. We'll give you the real number for your state on the call.

Your starting leads come from the Gulag at no cost to you. Later, aged leads are bought out of money you've already made.

How long until I'm actually making money?

Licensing usually runs a few weeks depending on your state and how fast you move through the coursework. Carrier contracting overlaps with it.

After that it depends almost entirely on your dial volume, and we're not going to pretend otherwise. Nobody can promise you a timeline or an income — this is commission only. What we can promise is that the leads, scripts, training and support are there from your first day, at no cost to you.

Can I do this part time?

People have started part time while transitioning out of a job, and that's a real conversation to have on the call.

What doesn't work is five hours a week, indefinitely. Leads are time-sensitive, the skill is built on volume, and part-time dialing tends to produce part-time reps and full-time frustration. Be straight with us about your hours and we'll be straight with you about whether it's worth your time.

What exactly am I selling?

Primarily mortgage protection — life insurance for homeowners so the mortgage doesn't fall on the family if something happens. You're working leads from people who requested information, not cold-calling strangers out of a phone book.

You'll be contracted with multiple carriers, so you're choosing the right fit for the client rather than forcing one product on everybody.
